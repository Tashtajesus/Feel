# nightcore-neon

Night sky, neon anime glow, Y2K sparkles. Dark violet ground; pink and cyan are the only
"voltage" colors and appear as glows, RGB-split ghosts and the visualizer ring. Lilac for
quiet stretches. White only for text and sparkles.

## Do
- Lowercase display type, huge, centered, inside the story safe zone (y 260–1580 on 1080×1920).
- Pink/cyan chromatic ghosts behind display type on snares; soft bloom (text-shadow) on active words.
- Four-point sparkles and tiny hearts as the ornament layer.
- Grain + faint scanlines + vignette over everything.

## Don't
- No pure black, no light backgrounds, no rainbow cycling.
- No text in the top 250px / bottom 340px (Instagram UI).
